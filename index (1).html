    How Blood Bridge works

    From sign-up to a donation that reaches a patient, in three simple parts.

    Donors register

    Volunteers sign up with their blood group, area and last donation date. Only the basics are stored.

    Families get matched

    A request finds eligible donors with a compatible group, starting with the patient's own area.

    A life is saved

    The donor gives at the blood bank and the team records it, so stock stays up to date.

    Request blood in an emergency

    Fill in the form. We check the hospital details, then alert eligible donors with a matching group, starting with those in the same area.

      Before you donate

      A short guide for first-time and returning donors.

      1. RegisterFill in a short form and show your ID.
      2. Quick health checkPulse, blood pressure and haemoglobin.
      3. DonateAbout 10 minutes, seated comfortably.
      4. Rest and refreshDrink water and have a snack for 15 minutes.

      You can donate if

      • You are 18 to 60 years old
      • You weigh at least 50 kg
      • You feel well today
      • It has been 3 months or more since your last donation

      Please wait if

      • You have a fever, cold or infection
      • You took antibiotics in the last week
      • You had a tattoo or surgery recently
      • You are pregnant or breastfeeding

      On the day

      • Eat a meal and drink plenty of water
      • Bring a photo ID
      • Rest for 15 minutes afterwards
      • Avoid heavy lifting until the next day

      This is general guidance. The blood bank's medical staff make the final decision on the day.
